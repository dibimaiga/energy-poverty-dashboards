Energy poverty dashboards

Two dashboards on access to electricity in Africa, built from public data. Every figure can be downloaded and traced back to its source, and the code that builds the data is open.

Senegal

Senegal: energy in numbers

Access to electricity in cities and rural areas, the power mix, electricity prices and outages, and the move to domestic gas.

Open the dashboard → Sub-Saharan Africa · 48 countries

Energy poverty scorecard

Countries ranked on seven criteria, from access and clean cooking to outages and oil-fired power, with weights you can adjust.

Open the scorecard →

Sources. World Bank World Development Indicators and Enterprise Surveys, Our World in Data (Energy Institute, Ember), Senelec tariffs published by Senegal's regulator (CRSE), GlobalPetrolPrices, and press reports for the gas milestones. Each dashboard lists its sources and links to its data files.

Independence. This is an independent analysis of public data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Prosperity, Not Poverty or any other organization.

Method and limits

Generation and mix. Our World in Data (Energy Institute, Ember), 2000-2024. Consumption is slightly higher than domestic generation; the gap is net electricity imports.

Access. World Bank indicators EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS (national), EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS (urban) and EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS (rural), from the SDG 7.1.1 dataset, updated on October 8, 2026. The government reports a national rate of 84%.

Prices. The official Senelec tariff grid approved by the regulator, CRSE (decision no. 2025-140), in force since January 1, 2026. The average household price comes from GlobalPetrolPrices (June 2025, all taxes included) and predates the 2026 cut.

Reliability. World Bank Enterprise Surveys (IC.ELC.OUTG.ZS), carried out every 5 to 10 years and in different years for each country. Losses: indicator EG.ELC.LOSS.ZS. The number of outages per month is no longer among the World Bank's WDI indicators.
